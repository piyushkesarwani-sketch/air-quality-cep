Air Quality Trend Analysis Using Open Environmental Data

Ghatkopar, Mumbai — an interactive presentation of the short-term environmental measurements and community perceptions documented in the CEP project.

Project at a glance

The study combines OpenAQ measurements from the Ghatkopar, Mumbai–BMC monitoring location with a 35-response community questionnaire. Environmental study window: 20–30 September 2026.

Main pollutants considered

Air-quality trends

Daily-average trend summaries from the project dataset.

PM2.5: generally decreasing during the selected period, with short-term fluctuations and a rise around 28–29 September.

PM10: fluctuated considerably and reached a reported peak daily average of 103.22 µg/m³ on 29 September.

NO₂: fluctuated through the period, reaching 17.57 ppb on 26 September and 15.38 ppb on 30 September.

O₃: comparatively stable, with a highest reported daily average of 4.12 µg/m³ on 29 September, followed by 3.24 µg/m³ on 30 September.

Community survey

The questionnaire contained 10 questions and received 35 responses.

Selected findings

  • 17 respondents (48.6%) rated local air quality as moderate.
  • 13 respondents (37.1%) noticed pollution often; another 13 said sometimes.
  • 21 respondents identified peak traffic hours as important.
  • 17 respondents selected construction activities as a major noticed source.
  • 15 respondents selected more trees/green spaces as an improvement.
  • Only 3 respondents reported checking AQI regularly.

Study areas

Ghatkopar, Vidyavihar and Kurla were the main planned areas; responses from other areas were retained as collected.

How the study was done

01 • Open data

OpenAQ measurements for the Ghatkopar, Mumbai–BMC monitoring location.

02 • Preparation

Records organized by pollutant, date, time, value and unit.

03 • Daily averages

Measurements grouped by date to create trend graphs.

04 • Survey

Google Forms questionnaire collected 35 responses.

05 • Comparison

Community perceptions considered alongside environmental indicators.

06 • Interpretation

Results treated as a short-term case study, not a definitive long-term trend.

Key findings

Environmental data

  • PM2.5 showed an overall downward movement with short-term fluctuations.
  • PM10 varied considerably and peaked on 29 September.
  • NO₂ showed noticeable fluctuations, with a peak on 26 September.
  • O₃ remained comparatively stable.

Community perspective

  • Moderate air quality was the most common perception.
  • Traffic hours and construction were frequently noticed.
  • Regular AQI checking was uncommon.
  • More trees and green spaces was the most selected improvement measure.

Important interpretation

The project uses an 11-day environmental observation window. Longer monthly, seasonal and annual datasets would be needed for stronger long-term trend conclusions.
